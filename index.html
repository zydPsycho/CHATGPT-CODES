ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

Organized like a real command system.

Commands are grouped by purpose, then divided into focused subcategories. Selecting a category never throws every command into one endless list: you choose a subcategory, browse a page, and move through the library with pagination.

GPT CODES are custom shorthand directives for use with normal instructions. They are not official OpenAI commands.
